Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common irrigation questions

A 2-wire decoder system uses just two wires to communicate with all irrigation valves, instead of running individual wires to each valve. Rain Bird and Hunter make industry-leading systems that reduce installation costs and simplify troubleshooting on large properties. We're certified specialists in both brands.

Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs) control pump motor speed to match water demand, saving energy and reducing wear. Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) automate complex irrigation sequences, monitor system health, and integrate with building management systems. We specialize in programming and maintaining these advanced automation systems.

Costs vary based on property size, soil conditions, and system complexity. A typical residential system ranges from $2,500-$6,000, while commercial systems can range from $10,000 to $100,000+. We provide free on-site estimates with detailed breakdowns and financing options for qualified customers.
